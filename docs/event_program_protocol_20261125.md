# Ten-Family Event Research Program

Decision date: 2026-11-25 (America/Denver). Host UTC timestamps may be November 26.

## Objective

Determine whether company-specific public information adds an executable, incremental
advantage beyond existing price strategies, particularly during leadership transitions.
This is not another sentiment-index screen. No source-access check, software test,
registered policy, synthetic fixture or retrospective narrative counts as an investment test.

## Frozen Common Design

- Long-only ordinary shares/ETFs and cash, no leverage, derivatives or automatic trading.
- Broad company studies require the existing qualified historical census, including failures,
  historical issuer/security identity intervals, adjusted open/close prices, cash accruals,
  explicit terminal settlements and independent price review. A narrow source pilot may run
  before this gate but cannot report tradable portfolio CAGR or imply broad stock evidence.
- Original release content and availability times are mandatory. Current-vintage fields,
  future restatements, current supply chains, present-day index constituents, final recall
  status and subsequent business outcomes cannot be used as if known historically.
- Public original release time may support a documented retrospective reconstruction; a
  capture made today is not a historical first-observed record. Version evidence must support
  whichever historical-availability assertion is made. Genuine prospective observations start
  at the first capture, never at a backdated article publication date.
- No historical text model with uncontrolled future-company knowledge. Start with transparent
  rules and return-blind content annotation; do not treat anonymization alone as proof that
  an LLM cannot leak future knowledge. Freeze extraction before evaluating investment returns.
- Predeclare three substantive formulations per family, for 30 specification slots. All are
  initially registered, not implemented or tested. Family-specific extraction, data review,
  event studies, portfolio execution, robustness and integration have separate states.
- Use 21/63/126-session response windows, including unfavorable and unmatched events in
  coverage counts. Primary horizon is 63 sessions; other horizons are diagnostics and count
  toward the search ledger. Do not drop unfavorable unmatched observations silently.
- Decisions use only then-known event and price information at a regular-session close.
  Execute at the next, second and fifth subsequent regular-session open. Initial price gaps
  before execution are reported separately and never credited as captured return.
- Replay real self-financing portfolios with 10/25/50 bps per side. Include combined
  five-session delay plus 25 bps and five-session delay plus 50 bps scenarios, not just
  one-factor stresses. Spread/liquidity evidence can require higher costs or exclusion.
- Treat negative events as avoidance/reallocation, not synthetic short returns. Compare with
  the same investable universe and costed neutral allocation. A stock-selection basket and a
  defensive avoidance policy answer different questions and have different matched controls.
- Controls: costed SPY; sector/size/momentum/liquidity matched then-known non-events; price-only;
  event-only; combined; exposure-matched controls fixed from development data. Do not fit
  control exposures using the evaluation interval. Do not claim independence across ETFs.
- Freeze chronological cutoffs after a return-blind coverage audit, before return evaluation.
  Target at least five development years, three validation years and two final diagnostic years;
  purge 126 sessions at boundaries. If fewer observations exist, scope a narrower pilot and
  call it underpowered, not a fully qualified broad strategy.
- Previously examined return periods remain diagnostic, not an unseen lockbox. A genuinely
  untouched holdout must be documented as such. Prospective records start from frozen specs.
- Use issuer/date clustered uncertainty and paired block portfolio intervals, account for the
  whole family search and selection process, and show leave-one-year/sector/issuer-out
  sensitivity. No event-average CAGR and no unadjusted winner p-value as proof of alpha.
- Minimum screening adequacy: 100 distinct issuer-event clusters across 30 issuers and five
  years for each promoted formulation. Passing this floor does not establish sufficient power;
  calculate detectable effect and report precision before calling a result negative. Sparse
  families remain unresolved and can use a predeclared sector scope instead of invented breadth.
- Primary promotion objective: at least 1 percentage point annual net incremental return
  without more than 2 points worse max drawdown, OR at least 5 points lower max drawdown with
  at most 1 point CAGR sacrifice. These are research screening preferences, not guarantees or
  sufficient admission conditions. Also show the full return/drawdown frontier and tail risk.
- Require temporal transport, cost/delay survival and contribution beyond price controls.
  Confidence intervals crossing zero yield a tentative/underpowered result, not proven alpha.
- At most two economically motivated follow-up rounds per family without a new protocol.
  New thresholds or filters after viewing outcomes consume fresh search budget; failed trials,
  parser revisions and all candidate/scenario evaluations stay in the ledger.
- Combine only surviving complementary families. Test pairs, then at most three-way
  combinations, with removal tests, equal-weight controls and turnover/exposure matching.
  Combination selection occurs on development/validation evidence, never test winners.
- Only after standalone survival: implement natively in the actual top Trade Bot strategy
  family and relevant Rotation Trader strategies; compare unchanged baselines and investigate
  whether apparent improvement is just reduced technology exposure. No post-hoc overlay claim.
- No source qualification, data review or retrospective result authorizes live allocation.

## Family Specifications

1. Guidance: own-prior revenue/margin/capex revisions; material revisions scaled by prior
   guidance; repeated revisions or confirmation. Separate cut/raise/withdrawal and the metric's
   economic meaning. No proprietary analyst-consensus substitute.
2. Cash conversion: same-period cash/earnings divergence; industry-relative receivables and
   inventory accumulation; persistent deterioration then verified repair. Do not mix quarterly,
   year-to-date and annual flows. New amendments become available only at their release.
3. Financing: issuance/covenant/going-concern events; severity scaled by then-known liquidity;
   financing relief and re-entry. No ex-post bankrupt-only sample.
4. Insider clusters: verified discretionary purchases; independent-buyer clusters and economic
   commitment; cluster plus existing price repair. Exclude grants/exercises/ambiguous owners,
   distinguish private/open-market purchases and use public filing rather than transaction time.
5. Capital returns: authorization versus disclosed execution; net share reduction after employee
   issuance; cash-funded versus debt-funded execution. Later execution never rewrites initial news.
6. Customer propagation: direct customer spending/contract change; historically documented
   exposure-scaled supplier propagation; diversified sector propagation. Freeze edges when known.
7. Disruptions: contemporaneous interruption categories; economically measured persistence;
   documented resolution/re-entry. Do not use final duration or recall termination status at onset.
8. Governance/accounting: auditor/restatement/control weakness/departure separately; documented
   adverse versus routine context; remediation/re-entry. Do not label departure harmful using
   subsequent returns or eventual fraud findings.
9. Regulatory/legal: initiation/decision/settlement/approval separately; outcome relative to
   explicitly prior public circumstances; company versus sector implementation. No invented surprise.
10. Original reporting: verified original company reporting versus syndication; incremental
    novelty beyond already-public official sources; cross-source corroboration and price controls.
    Avoid classifying ordinary press-release republication as independent new information.

## States And Stopping

Each family transitions through data feasibility, extraction validation, event study, executable
portfolio, robustness, native integration and prospective review. Record blocked, underpowered,
negative, tentative-positive and robust-research-positive separately. "Blocked" never means
"no edge". Rejected hypotheses and genuinely exhausted finite specification sets are different
from universal exhaustion of an economic idea. Missing data prevents performance conclusions.

Stop a tested family when adequate evidence shows no economically meaningful incremental effect,
the effect is untradeably fast, fails costs/delays or transport, or duplicates existing controls.
Stop additional tuning on that dataset after two unproductive predeclared follow-up rounds.
Stop source acquisition at denied access, unavailable authorization, licensing uncertainty or
unverifiable historical versions; record the exact condition for legitimate resumption.
