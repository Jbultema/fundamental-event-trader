# Event Research: Portfolio And New-Information Follow-Ups

November 26, 2026. **Conditional survivor-sample simulations, not validated investment
performance. No strategy admission, trading-policy change, purchase or live order.**

## Conclusion

The earlier event-return comparisons have now been taken through self-financing portfolio
accounting and adversarial controls. We also acquired five additional accounting fields and
tested three substantive mechanisms rather than tuning the first weak thresholds.

There is **no defensible performance breakthrough**. Some rules beat a weak price filter,
but the evidence does not establish incremental value over the full control set. Further
threshold optimization on this sample would be poor research discipline. This is not a
claim that event-driven investing or the original ten-family program has been exhausted.

The work totals **520 conditional portfolio paths**, including controls, transaction costs,
execution delays and cash assumptions. It is not 520 independent strategies or experiments:
there are 18 candidate policy specifications, three short-holding falsifiers, shared controls
and correlated stresses. The earlier 54 event-return scenarios remain a separate result set.

## Experiment 1: Actual Portfolio Accounting

- Same ten original companies; no return-selected replacements or universe expansion.
- Two accounting features, 21/63/126-session lives and event-only / daily SMA126-confirmed
  versions: 12 candidates. Plus buy-and-hold, price-only, all-disclosure, cash and two
  opposite-condition falsification controls.
- Fixed 10% target slots, no leverage, unused slots cash, holdings drift while targets are
  unchanged. A target change rebalances the basket. We reused the existing accounting engine.
- Decisions occur at the first regular close after the assumed date-only processing cutoff,
  with fills at the next, second or fifth regular open. This is one session later than the
  earlier midnight-to-next-open event-return convention, not a backdated close decision.
- 10/25/50 bps per side; zero interest primary cash assumption and predeclared 4% annual
  calendar-time cash sensitivities. These are assumptions, not observed money-fund returns.
- Development: January 3, 2023-June 28, 2024. Then 128 trading sessions excluded from parameter
  fitting/performance selection. Evaluation: January 2, 2025-November 25, 2026. These periods
  were previously inspected; this is not a new untouched holdout.
- Each candidate has a neutral control sized using its development average realized equity
  exposure. That estimate is not fit to evaluation returns or evaluation exposure. Its later
  actual exposure can differ from the candidate; this is not an exact ex-post beta match.
- 330 portfolio paths: 30 candidate/control paths across 11 execution/cash scenarios.

Evaluation results below use one-session execution, 25 bps per side and zero cash interest.
These are calendar-period, carried-position **simulated portfolio CAGR and maximum drawdown**,
not annualized event averages and not a claim about Trade Bot's actual top strategies.

| Same Ten-Stock Study | CAGR | Max DD | Average Equity Exposure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Neutral equal-weight-initialized buy-and-hold | 18.86% | -14.92% | 100.0% |
| Net common repurchases, 126 sessions | 17.42% | -13.87% | 96.7% |
| Net common repurchases, 63 sessions | 14.74% | -14.29% | 91.8% |
| All disclosures, 63 sessions | 16.74% | -13.75% | 96.3% |
| Cash-conversion improvement, 126 sessions | 9.77% | -8.83% | 65.6% |
| Its development-fitted exposure control | 9.88% | -8.54% | 59.6% |
| Cash-conversion improvement, 63 sessions | 6.22% | -6.26% | 47.7% |
| Its development-fitted exposure control | 8.31% | -7.17% | 50.5% |
| Price-only daily SMA126 | 5.16% | -11.50% | 63.1% |

The 126-session repurchase rule mostly approximated buy-and-hold. It beat its development-
fitted exposure control, but later held more equity and did not beat neutral buy-and-hold.
The 63-session all-disclosure control also beat the 63-session positive repurchase rule.
For cash conversion, the opposite-condition 63-session falsifier returned 9.76% CAGR versus
6.22% for the positive rule, at reasonably similar average exposures (50.6% versus 47.7%).
That is a warning about directional interpretation, not permission to reverse the rule.

The specific daily SMA confirmation was costly. For the 63-session repurchase policy it
reduced CAGR from 14.74% to 2.98% and increased annual notional turnover from 4.17x to 12.70x.
The unconfirmed version generated 484 trade lines across 54 trading days during evaluation;
confirmation generated 1,115 lines across 184 days. This is important for human execution.
It is a result about this filter and this sample, not evidence against every trend strategy.

Under five-session execution plus 50 bps per side, the longer repurchase policy returned
16.19% CAGR / -14.62% DD versus neutral 18.82% / -14.94%. Cash conversion at 126 sessions
returned 8.63% / -10.22%. Adding the fixed 4% cash assumption did not reverse the main
finding: the attractive high-return rule was largely invested, while selective rules gave
up substantial participation without demonstrated incremental timing value.

## Experiment 2: New Accounting Information

We downloaded receivables, inventory, revenue, total debt and cash/equivalents for the same
ten companies. The original financial inputs and price paths were required to reconcile
exactly to the earlier acquisition; no changed vintage was silently substituted.

The source protocol fixed the new mechanisms before downloading these fields. The native
code and portfolio protocol were sealed before evaluating their returns. Prior sample
returns were already known, so none of this is described as a new unseen holdout.

1. **Working-capital repair:** receivables plus inventory / trailing-year revenue improves
   versus an earlier released prior-year observation; revenue does not decline and income
   is positive. This is an accounting proxy, not validated interpretation of an 8-K event.
2. **Self-funded net repurchases:** net common-stock financing is negative, income is positive,
   operating cash flow covers those net repurchases, and total debt is not higher than in
   the earlier report. Cash-flow coverage does not prove how fungible cash was allocated.
3. **Liquidity repair:** net cash minus debt / assets improves, debt / assets declines, and
   operating cash flow is positive. This does not substitute for extracting discrete
   financing, covenant, going-concern or relief events from original disclosures.

All compare trailing-year as-reported observations, with prior report periods 300-430 days
earlier and only earlier public filing dates. Missing data do not turn into zero or a
positive signal. Unrelated missing inventory would not disable a valid funding feature.

JPM was retained in all downloads but excluded from all three mechanisms before outcomes,
because bank cash-flow, working-capital and debt accounting is not comparable. Its original
10% target slot stays cash in each neutral/price/disclosure control as well. Initial cash
and equity weights can later drift. The study is therefore not directly interchangeable
with the original ten-stock comparison above.

There are six candidates (63/126-session lives), three additional 21-session falsifiers,
four neutral controls and six development-fitted exposure controls: 19 paths across ten
cost/delay/cash scenarios, or 190 additional conditional paths. Short-life controls are
reported so the earlier poor short-horizon behavior is not omitted from this follow-up.
Signals were not combined after failing standalone qualification.

The 600 feature rows comprise 432 observed rows, 108 explicit prior-year left-censored rows
and 60 bank-excluded rows. Positive release counts are 68 working-capital, 59 self-funded
repurchase and 70 liquidity observations. These are repeated-company events, not independent
issuer clusters.

Same evaluation period, one-session delay, 25 bps per side, zero cash-interest assumption:

| Nonfinancial-Scope Study | CAGR | Max DD | Average Equity Exposure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Eligible neutral basket, initial JPM slot cash | 17.64% | -13.07% | 93.7% |
| Working-capital repair, 126 sessions | 9.59% | -8.23% | 49.1% |
| Its development-fitted exposure control | 10.02% | -8.21% | 60.7% |
| Self-funded repurchases, 126 sessions | 6.81% | -10.34% | 48.8% |
| Its development-fitted exposure control | 7.28% | -5.94% | 44.5% |
| Liquidity repair, 126 sessions | 9.77% | -10.27% | 60.0% |
| Its development-fitted exposure control | 8.72% | -7.13% | 53.1% |
| Eligible price-only SMA126 | 5.28% | -9.11% | 55.0% |

Working-capital repair is the most plausible topic for a broader independent study: it
beats this price-only filter at lower exposure, but does not clearly beat the neutral
development-fitted exposure control. Self-funded repurchases have poor incremental results.
Liquidity repair's roughly one-point CAGR gain over its exposure control comes with more
than three points worse drawdown, outside the frozen screening preference.

## Uncertainty And Regime Checks

The runs use paired calendar-block bootstrap intervals (2,000 repetitions; 21- and 63-session
blocks), preserving common candidate/control dates. A max-absolute-mean null diagnostic is
computed across the current candidates. This is not correction for all historical Trade Bot
research, all ideas considered, survivor selection or the choice of this sample.

At one-session / 25 bps execution, every new mechanism's 63-session-block interval for
annualized arithmetic excess versus its development-fitted exposure control includes zero.
For the longer working-capital rule, the estimate is -0.45 percentage points, with an
approximate interval of -5.77 to +8.93 points. The longer original repurchase rule also has
an interval crossing zero against its exposure control, despite its higher point return.
The data are underpowered; this is not proof that the economic ideas have no value.

A separate post-result diagnostic labels stress only from the previous close: neutral-
basket drawdown at least 10% or 21-session annualized realized volatility at least 25%.
Recent repair means stress occurred within the prior 21 sessions and is no longer active.
No future return or full-path trough determines a label. These labels do not trigger any
strategy, and they do not measure advance warning of a break.

Evaluation contains only **24 already-stressed sessions**, 21 recent-repair sessions and
432 ordinary sessions. This is far too little independent crisis history to claim durable
protection in an AI-led unwind or to assess fall-2000-type transitions. Some quality rules
had smaller worst daily losses but lagged during ordinary sessions. A positive average
return while labeled stressed can reflect rebound days after damage, not successful crash
prediction. There is still no new 2000/2008 company-event backtest.

## What We Can Conclude

- Generic profitable-company net buybacks were too common here to be a strong timing signal.
- Reduced drawdown from a selective rule is not itself evidence of useful event information;
  participation, cash exposure and neutral controls must be shown beside it.
- The tested daily confirmation filter materially increased turnover and execution burden.
- Working-capital repair merits better data more than additional threshold searching.
- There is no surviving standalone event rule ready to combine, promote, or integrate into
  the actual top Trade Bot strategies from these results.

These statements apply to this conditional sample. Do not generalize them to all regimes,
all companies or all event strategies. The original guidance, insider-cluster, customer,
disruption, governance, legal and original-reporting families remain unvalidated; a
balance-sheet repair proxy is not completion of the original financing-event family either.

## Next Research Gate

The next substantive improvement is a broader historical dataset, not more tuning here:
failed/delisted companies, stable then-known identities, original release/version evidence,
adjusted open/close paths and verified terminal settlements. The original news/event
families also need their own validated extractors and authorized historical source archives.
The [provider comparison](event_data_decision_20261126.md) remains a procurement option,
not purchase authorization or data qualification. A subscription would not instantly solve
all of these tasks. No raw-data retention or redistribution right should be assumed.
