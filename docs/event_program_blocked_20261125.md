# Event Research Goal: Blocked, Not Exhausted

Date: November 25, 2026, America/Denver.

## Decision

The full ten-family investment-research objective is not achieved. After three consecutive
goal turns, the same blocking condition remains: no qualified historical event/security/price/
terminal-settlement dataset is available. Acquisition and content extraction advanced, but
cannot establish investment performance. Further implementation-only iterations would not
answer whether any hypothesis adds an executable trading advantage.

The correct goal status is blocked. This is neither an economic rejection of the hypotheses
nor proof that all possible public datasets or research mechanisms have been exhausted.

## Evidence Revalidated

- The ten-family protocol registers thirty initial formulations; it does not implement or
  backtest thirty strategies. The original news-index ETF experiments remain separate.
- A pinned public corpus supplied 70,672 source filing rows. The final content pilot parsed or
  indexed 68,966 accessions from 2,357 issuer CIKs, rejected 1,706 rows with explicit reasons,
  and extracted 43,042 non-derivative purchase/sale transaction rows. No returns were evaluated.
- Historical listing snapshots and a delisted-security listing were acquired, but contain no
  permanent-security/CIK mapping or terminal investment payouts. Duplicate symbols remain
  unresolved. A dated list is not a complete adjusted daily return panel.
- The configured Alpha Vantage adjusted-daily request for a fixed delisted-company diagnostic
  returned an Information payload indicating premium-only access. No purchase was authorized.
- Final Yahoo and Stooq public price-route checks found publisher robots exclusions. The
  requested price URLs were not downloaded, positive-control requests were skipped after the
  host-level exclusions, and no browser identity, proxy or other workaround was used.
- SEC acquisition denials, CourtListener authorization, article-archive permissions and
  historical publication/version evidence remain unresolved where those sources are required.
- Source/report hashes and acquisition byte lengths were verified. The local software suite
  has 230 passed, zero failures/errors/skips. These are not investment trials or remote CI.
- There is no new qualified event dataset in the native data directory. Pilot filenames and
  schemas deliberately do not impersonate the required events/identity/price/terminal tables.

## What Unblocks The Goal

Supply a legitimate existing local archive or authorize access to a suitable public-market
data service that can satisfy the historical identity, daily adjusted price, corporate-action
and terminal-settlement requirements. File/event release and article-use qualifications must
also be completed for each family. Provider names or local paths are sufficient to investigate;
credentials should not be pasted into chat. No new subscription or purchase was made.

Once those inputs are available, resume data review, family-specific extraction and the
frozen event-study/portfolio protocol. Unseen/prospective evidence must remain distinct from
the historical periods already examined in prior Trade Bot research. No gate is relaxed simply
because a survivor-only or revised-history backtest would be easier to run.
